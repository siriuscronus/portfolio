# PMO & Portfolio Governance Frameworks

This section documents practical management structures for complex technology portfolios and programs.

## Governance Model

A strong governance model connects strategy, investment, execution, risk, and measurable outcomes.

### Portfolio Level

- Strategic alignment
- Investment prioritization
- Capacity planning
- Benefits management
- Portfolio risk
- Executive governance

### Program Level

- Integrated planning
- Cross-project dependencies
- Financial control
- Stakeholder alignment
- RAID governance
- Benefits realization

### Project Level

- Scope
- Schedule
- Cost
- Quality
- Resources
- Risks
- Issues
- Change control

## Executive Dashboard Structure

A concise executive dashboard should answer:

1. Are we on track?
2. What changed?
3. What requires a decision?
4. What threatens business outcomes?
5. What financial exposure exists?
6. What happens next?

Recommended indicators:

- Overall health
- Schedule variance
- Cost variance
- Milestone status
- Top risks
- Critical issues
- Decisions required
- Benefits status
- Resource constraints
- Forecast

## RAID Governance

Risk, Assumption, Issue, and Dependency management should include:

- Owner
- Probability
- Impact
- Exposure
- Mitigation
- Due date
- Escalation threshold
- Current status

## RACI

RACI remains useful when it clarifies decision ownership. It becomes counterproductive when every task receives an excessive number of stakeholders.

Use it primarily for:

- Critical decisions
- Cross-functional processes
- Governance forums
- Major deliverables
- Escalation paths

## Stage Gates

Typical stage gates:

- Opportunity / Intake
- Business Case
- Planning
- Execution Readiness
- Delivery
- Acceptance
- Closure
- Benefits Review

Each gate should have objective entry and exit criteria.
