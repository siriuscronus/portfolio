# Regional Telecom, Cloud & Infrastructure Portfolio

**Organization:** Huawei  
**Roles:** Project Manager / Fulfillment Responsible Project Director  
**Period:** 2018–2024  
**Coverage:** Latin America  
**Portfolio Scale:** Approximately USD 90M  
**Domain:** Telecommunications, Cloud, Infrastructure, Technology Delivery

## Executive Summary

Led regional, multi-country delivery across telecommunications, cloud, and infrastructure programs with an approximate portfolio value of USD 90M.

The role required coordination across engineering, commercial, operations, finance, customers, suppliers, and executive stakeholders while maintaining visibility over delivery, risk, SLA performance, billing, and business outcomes.
