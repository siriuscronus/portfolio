# National Broadband Program

**Role:** Program Manager  
**Period:** 2016  
**Geography:** Nicaragua  
**Program Scale:** USD 39.7M  
**Domain:** National Broadband, Digital Infrastructure, Telecommunications

## Executive Summary

Directed a USD 39.7M national broadband program funded by international organizations, with responsibilities spanning architecture, design, governance, procurement strategy, stakeholder coordination, and delivery planning.

The program required the integration of public-sector objectives, engineering design, procurement, institutional governance, and execution frameworks into a single national infrastructure initiative.

## Program Context

National broadband programs involve more than telecommunications deployment. They require alignment between:

- Public policy objectives
- Network architecture
- Technology choices
- Procurement strategy
- Funding requirements
- Institutional stakeholders
- Delivery governance
- Long-term operational objectives

As Program Manager, I served as a key leadership resource across architecture, design, governance, and coordination.
