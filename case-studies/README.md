## Case Study 5. WaveNexus Technology Product Program

### Product

### Program Leadership Scope

- Product vision and scope definition
- Multi-phase roadmap management
- Product backlog and requirements decomposition
- Architecture coordination
- Engineering governance
- Release planning
- QA and verification strategy
- Build and test evidence
- Version governance
- Risk management
- CI/CD-oriented development workflow
- AI-assisted product development

### Delivery Model

The development process uses phased cumulative builds, verification checkpoints, regression testing, evidence packages, and controlled version progression.

### Executive Relevance

The initiative demonstrates the intersection of:

- Product management
- Technical program management
- Engineering governance
- Software delivery
- Telecommunications domain expertise
- AI-assisted development
