# National Digital Infrastructure Strategy, Market Intelligence & Regulatory Modernization

**Organization:** Avasant  
**Role:** Project Director  
**Period:** 2018  
**Geography:** Nicaragua  
**Domain:** Telecommunications, Broadband, Digital Infrastructure, Regulation

## Executive Summary

Directed a national-level telecommunications consulting engagement designed to strengthen long-term broadband planning and strategic decision-making. The work connected market intelligence, demand forecasting, national telecommunications architecture, operator benchmarking, institutional coordination, and regulatory modernization.

The engagement required translating complex engineering, commercial, market, and regulatory information into structured decision support for national stakeholders.

## Strategic Context

Long-term broadband development requires more than network expansion. It depends on a coordinated view of demand, operator capabilities, infrastructure architecture, regulatory conditions, investment priorities, and institutional objectives.

The engagement addressed these dimensions as an integrated strategy problem.

## Scope

Key workstreams included:

- ICT and broadband market assessment
- 10-year demand outlook for fixed and mobile broadband services
- Benchmarking of Internet service operators and market capabilities
- Analysis of national telecommunications infrastructure and architecture
- Development of a management approach and tool for national telecommunications architecture
- Strategic recommendations for long-term broadband development
- Multi-stakeholder coordination across public-sector institutions
- Review and modernization of the telecommunications regulatory framework
- Decision-support analysis for national digital infrastructure planning

## My Role

As Project Director, I coordinated the execution of the consulting work and the interaction between technical, market, regulatory, and institutional workstreams.

My responsibilities included:

- Structuring the engagement around executive decision needs
- Coordinating multidisciplinary analysis
- Aligning technical architecture with market and policy considerations
- Facilitating stakeholder alignment across institutions
- Supporting recovery and execution of a delayed national initiative
- Converting detailed findings into actionable recommendations
