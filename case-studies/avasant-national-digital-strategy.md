# National Digital Infrastructure Strategy, Market Intelligence, Network Management Platform & Regulatory Modernization

**Organization:** Avasant  
**Role:** Project Director | Solution / Product Architect  
**Period:** 2018  
**Geography:** Nicaragua  
**Domain:** Telecommunications, Broadband, Digital Infrastructure, Software Product Architecture, Regulation

## Executive Summary

Directed a national-level telecommunications consulting engagement designed to strengthen long-term broadband planning and strategic decision-making. The work connected market intelligence, demand forecasting, national telecommunications architecture, operator benchmarking, institutional coordination, software product development, and regulatory modernization.

A major component of the engagement was the design and development of a telecommunications network management platform intended to consolidate and manage information across operators, network domains, and technologies at national scale.

I served as the Solution / Product Architect for this platform and managed the development effort, translating national telecommunications-management requirements into a structured product architecture and development scope.

## Strategic Context

Long-term broadband development requires more than network expansion. It depends on a coordinated view of demand, operator capabilities, infrastructure architecture, regulatory conditions, investment priorities, and institutional objectives.

The engagement addressed these dimensions as an integrated strategy problem and added a software-based management capability to support national telecommunications visibility and planning.
